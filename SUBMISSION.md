## Description (3 sentences)

Plug is pay-per-sale advertising for AI agents: sellers fund campaigns, agents recommend offers labelled Sponsored, and commissions are paid only when referrals produce real on-chain purchases. Plug uses Arc for USDC campaign escrow and an atomic seller/agent split that pays the seller the full purchase price and the agent a commission from the seller's advertising budget in one transaction. Arc Studio built the contracts, the 89/89 passing Foundry tests including invariants, the max-preset audit, and the app.
